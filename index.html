Safe zones for Stories, Reels and Shorts

A 9:16 canvas is never shown edge to edge. The app draws the profile name and progress bar over the top 250 px, and the reply field, like button, captions and sound tag over the bottom 340 px. Anything you place there, a logo, a price, a call to action, will be covered on most phones. Keep essential content in the middle 1330 px and treat the bands as pure background. Creative Resize positions caption text inside the safe area on vertical formats automatically, and the "Show safe zones" switch overlays the bands on every 9:16 preview so you can check your crop before exporting.

Why smart crop beats stretching

Stretching a 3:2 poster into a 9:16 Story squeezes faces, warps logos and makes type look amateur; it is the one thing viewers notice immediately. Smart crop keeps the pixels undistorted: the image is scaled until it covers the target size and the overflow is cut away. The weakness of automatic cropping is that it usually cuts around the center, which is rarely where the subject is. That is what the focal point solves. Drag the dot onto the face, the product or the headline in the source thumbnail and every format, square, portrait, landscape and vertical, keeps that point inside the frame. When one placement still needs a nudge, open its preview and drag the image or zoom in; the override applies only to that format and is remembered until you reset it.

The blurred-background trick

Some designs cannot be cropped, for example a poster with text on every edge or a product shot that must stay complete. Switch to Fit + blur: the full image is scaled to fit inside the target, and the empty area is filled with a blurred, slightly darkened copy of the same image. The colors match perfectly, so it reads as an intentional layout rather than black bars, and it is the technique news outlets and music apps use for vertical video. Adjust the blur radius, add padding and round the corners of the inner image for a card look. Fit + color does the same with a flat background, sampled automatically from the dominant color of your image or picked manually to match your brand.

Frequently asked questions

What size should an Instagram Story or Reel be?

1080 × 1920 pixels (9:16). The same size works for TikTok videos, YouTube Shorts and WhatsApp Status. Keep logos, text and calls to action out of the top 250 px and the bottom 340 px, where the profile header, reply bar and buttons are drawn over your image. Creative Resize places captions inside those safe zones automatically and can draw the zones on the previews.

Is Creative Resize free?

Yes. All 14 formats, one custom size, every fill mode, the focal point, captions and PNG, JPG or WebP export are free with no sign-up. Free exports carry a small "Creative Resize · free" badge in the corner and download up to 4 formats at a time. The one-time Pro upgrade removes the badge, bundles every format into a ZIP, adds 2x export, unlimited custom sizes, saved format bundles and batch processing of several images.

Do you upload my images?

No. Every format is rendered with the HTML5 Canvas API inside your browser. The image never leaves your device, nothing is stored on a server, and the tool keeps working offline once the page has loaded.

How does smart crop work?

Smart crop scales your image until it covers the target size and then cuts away the overflow. Instead of always cropping around the center, it centers each crop on the focal point you drag onto the source thumbnail, so a face, product or headline stays in frame in the square, portrait, landscape and vertical versions. In the focus view you can pan and zoom any single format without affecting the others.

What is the blurred background option?

Fit + blurred background keeps your whole design visible, scaled to fit inside the target size, and fills the empty space with a blurred, slightly darkened copy of the same image. It is the standard trick for turning a landscape design into a Story or a vertical poster into a YouTube thumbnail without cropping anything. You control the blur radius, padding and the corner radius of the inner image.

Can I resize many images at once?

With Pro you can drop several images at the same time. Each one is resized to every selected format using the same settings, and all results download as one ZIP with descriptive file names such as instagram-story-reels-1080x1920. The free version resizes one image at a time.
